Frequently Asked Questions


1. How do I book a trip through Peregrine Travels?

We curate top travel destinations and provide direct access links to verified booking portals for flights, hotels, trains, buses, cabs, and rental vehicles. Simply browse our pages and click through to finalize your booking directly.



2. Does Peregrine Travels charge any extra booking fees?

No, we do not charge users any hidden service or booking fees. Our platform is completely free to explore and use for travel inspiration and booking navigation.



3. Can corporates and travel agencies partner with you?

Yes, we welcome collaborations with travel management companies, tour operators, and hospitality brands. You can reach out to our corporate desk at partnerships@peregrinetravels.com.
